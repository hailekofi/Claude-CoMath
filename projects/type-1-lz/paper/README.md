# paper/ — research workspace

Active working area for the Type-1 LZ project, organized into four buckets:

- **`program/`** — navigation & planning: research overview, the research program,
  the open problem statement, attack lines, restart structure, and the current-status
  reference (`where_we_are.tex`).
- **`drafts/`** — formal `.tex` writeups being assembled: the living working paper,
  strategy memo, accessory-node letter, and the Magnus–Feynman geometry primer.
- **`notes/`** — result & topic notes (the "what we found" derivations and writeups),
  plus the KZ-line peer review.
- **`working_sessions/`** — the `ws_*` delegated-session logs: geometry milestones
  M1–M6, workstreams a/c/d/e/g/o2/o2b/o3, and the a-flow line.

Deliverable paths cited in `../RESEARCH_LOG.md` resolve into these buckets.
