\begin{abstract}
We organize the Type-1 $N{=}3$ multistate Landau--Zener (MLZ) transition matrix $P$ as the squared
modulus of the Stokes/connection data of a rank-2 irregular meromorphic connection on the model's
\emph{spectral curve}. The central structural facts, established here at machine precision, are:
(i) the commuting Type-1 family is a \emph{ring} whose time-quadratic partner reduces to the linear
family and which shares a single eigenbasis, so the integrable structure fixes only the
\emph{Abelian} data (adiabatic spectrum, WKB phases, the Brundobler--Elser survivals) and cannot
reach the non-Abelian prefactor; and (ii) the spectral curve $\Sigma:\det(E-H(u))=0$ is
\emph{genus-0 rational}, carrying three regular poles $\{\eps_i\}$, an irregular point at $u=\infty$,
and a \emph{structural node} --- a genuine eigenvalue crossing. Consequently the transition
amplitudes belong to the genus-0 confluent-Heun / Kamp\'e de F\'eriet class (not elliptic or
Painlev\'e), with the slope dependence entering linearly through the dynamical phases on a fixed
geometric connection. The open content --- the middle survival $\Pmid$ and one off-diagonal --- is
the connection coefficient threading the turning points and the node; numerically it dominates the
naive incoherent product by $2.5$--$104\times$. We state the resulting research program, predictions,
and the domain where the answer is expected to collapse to elementary form.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The model is $H(u)=H_0+uA$, $A=\mathrm{diag}(a_i)$, with the Cauchy/Type-1 coupling
$(H_0)_{ij}=\gamma_i\gamma_j(a_i-a_j)/(\eps_i-\eps_j)$; the observable is the doubly-stochastic
$P_{x\to j}=|f_j(+\infty)|^2$ on $4$ physical parameters. Two extreme-slope survivals are exactly
known (Brundobler--Elser); the middle survival and one off-diagonal are open.

\section{Framework}
\subsection{$P$ as connection/Stokes data}
The ODE $i\,\psi'(u)=(H_0+uA)\psi$ has a rank-2 irregular singularity at $u=\infty$; for $N{=}2$ it
is Weber's equation and the LZ factor is its Stokes multiplier. Thus $P_{ij}=|S_{ij}|^2$ with $S$
the connection across $\infty$. \pnote{F1, analytically-derived; see paper/kz\_isomonodromy\_picture.md}

\subsection{The commuting ring and the Abelian ceiling}
The family $\{H^{(a)}(u)\}$ (fixed $\gamma,\eps$) is a commutative ring spanning the full commutant;
the time-quadratic partner satisfies $H(u)^2=\sum_k c_k(u)H^{(a_k)}(u)$ with $c_k$ linear in $u$, and
the family shares one eigenbasis $\phi_i(u)$. \pnote{E2, established; experiments/ring\_structure.py}
Hence $G^{(a)}(u)=\mathrm{diag}(E^{(a)})-iW$, $W_{ij}=\langle\phi_i|\phi_j'\rangle$ \emph{a-independent},
$E^{(a)}$ \emph{a-linear}, so $P^{(a)}=|\text{holonomy of fixed }W\text{ twisted by a-linear phases}|^2$.
\pnote{E4, established} A commuting partner therefore yields only Abelian data and cannot produce the
prefactor. \pnote{viability verdict, analytically-derived}

\subsection{The spectral curve is genus-0 with a node (the exact crossing)}
$\Sigma:\chi_H(E,u)=0$ admits the global rational parametrization $E=m(\lambda)/p(\lambda)$,
$u=n(\lambda)/p(\lambda)$ $\Rightarrow$ genus $0$. \pnote{E3, established; paper/gate\_test\_genus.md}
The discriminant $D(u)=\mathrm{Disc}_E\chi_H$ is a sextic with multiplicities $[1,1,1,1,2]$; the
double root has trivial monodromy --- a \emph{node}, a real exact eigenvalue crossing $u_*$ --- while
the four simple roots are genuine branch points (avoided crossings). The genus-1 curve
$\mu^2=Q_4$ is the WKB \emph{phase} double-cover, whose periods residue-collapse to the elementary
BE exponents; it is not $\Sigma$. \pnote{reconciliation, established}

\section{Predictions}
P-i: the prefactor peaks/diverges as parameters approach the node $u_*$ (near-node stratum).
P-ii: on any factorization locus the matrix collapses to an elementary product to $10^{-9}$ (sharp
bimodal test). P-iii: the off-diagonal carries an interference phase that is a \emph{relative}
period between the two windows, not a closed window period.
\pnote{all falsifiable against the $10^{-9}$ oracle}

\section{Domain of validity}
Applies to generic Type-1 $N{=}3$ with distinct slopes and nonzero couplings (couplings never vanish
$\Rightarrow$ the Chernyak--Sinitsyn bow-tie solvable point lies \emph{outside} Type-1).
\pnote{paper/anchor\_experiment.md} Expected elementary degenerations: extreme survivals (BE), and any
sub-locus where the $3{\times}3$ $\lambda$-connection factorizes (WS-C). Breaks (hardest regime):
strong crossing overlap, where the prefactor is largest.

\section{Discussion}
The program (\texttt{paper/RESEARCH\_PROGRAM.md}) computes the connection coefficient (WS-A,E),
tests node reduction (WS-B) and factorization (WS-C), and searches for a non-commuting zero-curvature
operator that would force an exact product (WS-D). Two structural theorems hold regardless of the
prefactor's closed form: the Abelian ceiling (R2) and the rational-curve-with-node (R3).
