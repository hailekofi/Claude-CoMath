\title{The minimal non-factorizable multistate Landau--Zener problem:\\
Type-1 $N{=}3$ and the confluent-Heun Stokes coefficient}
\author{Co-theoretical-physicist working session}
\affiliation{\texttt{projects/type-1-lz}; branch \texttt{claude/comathematician-agent-skills-9HPSJ}}
\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
Exactly solvable multistate Landau--Zener (MLZ) models form a ``constellation'' of disconnected
results with little governing theory. We give a structural account of one boundary of that
constellation. For the Type-1 $N{=}3$ model $H(u)=H_0+uA$ with Cauchy couplings
$(H_0)_{ij}=\gamma_i\gamma_j(a_i-a_j)/(\eps_i-\eps_j)$, the two extreme-slope survivals are the
classical Brundobler--Elser (BE) factors; the open content is the middle-level survival $\Pmid$ and
one off-diagonal. We show: (i) the integrable (commuting-ring) structure is \emph{Abelian} and fixes
only the spectral data (BE survivals, WKB phases), never the transition amplitudes; (ii) the
spectral curve is genus-0 rational and carries a \emph{universal real exact level crossing}; (iii)
the avoided-crossing widths are \emph{slope-independent}, $w_{ij}=|2s_{ij}|=2|\gamma_i\gamma_j|/|\eps_i-\eps_j|$,
forcing the crossings to be permanently marginally overlapping, so no exact factorization
$\Scat=\prod \Scat_{ij}$ exists; (iv) the irreducible content is the holonomy of a single $12{\times}13$
spectral-network \emph{joint}, equal to the off-diagonal Stokes coefficient of a rank-2 irregular
point of a genuinely three-level system --- a genus-0 confluent-Heun object \emph{one accessory
parameter above} the ${}_1F_2$/Kamp\'e de F\'eriet of the solvable (tridiagonal) cousins; and (v) a
sharp classifier, \emph{elementary $\Leftrightarrow$ a level decouples}. Type-1 $N{=}3$ is thus the
minimal full (non-tridiagonal), confluent MLZ system whose middle survival is an irreducible
confluent-Heun connection coefficient. All structural claims are verified at machine precision or
against a $10^{-9}$ benchmark; the generic value of $\Pmid$ is the named-but-unevaluated connection
coefficient, which we pose as the open problem.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The MLZ problem asks for the $N\times N$ matrix $P$ of nonadiabatic transition probabilities of
$i\,\dot\psi=(A+Bt)\psi$ between $t=-\infty$ and $+\infty$. Solvable cases (Demkov--Osherov,
generalized bow-tie, Tavis--Cummings) are isolated and share a feature we make precise: their
spectral networks are \emph{joint-free}. Type-1 is the integrable family
$H(u)=H_0+uA$, $A=\mathrm{diag}(a_i)$, with the Cauchy/Gaudin coupling
$(H_0)_{ij}=\gamma_i\gamma_j(a_i-a_j)/(\eps_i-\eps_j)$ and
$(H_0)_{ii}=-\sum_{k\ne i}\gamma_k^2(a_i-a_k)/(\eps_i-\eps_k)$; the observable
$P_{x\to j}=|f_j(+\infty)|^2$ is doubly stochastic on four physical parameters. The two
extreme-slope survivals are the BE law $P=\prod_m e^{-2\pi\, s_{nm}^2|a_n-a_m|}$, where
$s_{ij}=\gamma_i\gamma_j/(\eps_i-\eps_j)$ is the signed Cauchy half-width
($w_{ij}=|2s_{ij}|$) and $s_{ij}^2|a_i-a_j|$ the BE pairwise exponent. The middle survival $\Pmid$
and one off-diagonal are open. \pnote{model+BE from project brief; standard}

\section{Framework: $P$ as Stokes data}
$i\,\psi'(u)=(H_0+uA)\psi$ has entire coefficients, hence a \emph{single} irregular singular point at
$u=\infty$ of Poincar\'e rank $2$ (for $N{=}2$ this is Weber's equation and the LZ factor is its
Stokes multiplier). Thus $P_{ij}=|\Scat_{ij}|^2$, with $\Scat$ the scattering connection across
$\infty$.\pnote{F1, AD; kz\_isomonodromy\_picture.md} The Chernyak--Sinitsyn $\tau$-deformations that preserve $P$ are
isomonodromic. The whole question is the off-diagonal of this single irregular connection.

\section{Results}

\subsection{The integrable structure is Abelian (a ceiling)}
\textbf{R1 [EST].} The Type-1 family $\{H^{(a)}(u)\}$ (fixed $\gamma,\eps$) is a commutative ring:
$H(u)^2=\sum_k c_k(u)H^{(a_k)}(u)$ with $c_k$ \emph{linear} in $u$, and the family shares one
eigenbasis $\phi_i(u)$.\pnote{experiments/ring\_structure.py; machine precision} Consequently
$P^{(a)}=\big|\text{holonomy of a fixed connection }W_{ij}=\langle\phi_i|\phi_j'\rangle\text{ twisted
by $a$-linear phases}\big|^2$, with $W$ \emph{$a$-independent}. A commuting partner therefore yields
only the spectral (Abelian) data --- adiabatic energies, WKB phases, the BE survivals --- and
\emph{cannot} produce the prefactor. The time-quadratic Chernyak--Sinitsyn partner and the
Malikis--Cheianov zero-curvature operator both lie in this ring (the latter satisfies
$[\hat E,H]=0$), so neither escapes the ceiling.\pnote{ws\_d\_nonabelian\_E.md; verified}

\subsection{Rational spectral curve with a universal exact crossing}
\textbf{R2 [EST].} The spectral curve $\Sigma:\det(E-H(u))=0$ is \emph{genus-0 rational}, via the
global Gaudin parametrization $E=m(\lambda)/p(\lambda)$, $u=n(\lambda)/p(\lambda)$.\pnote{gate\_test\_genus.md}
The discriminant $D(u)=\mathrm{Disc}_E\chi_H$ is a sextic with multiplicities $[1,1,1,1,2]$: four
simple complex branch points (the complexified avoided crossings) and one \emph{node} of trivial
monodromy --- a real exact level crossing (exact for canonical via a symbolic double root of $D$).
\textbf{R3 [NS].} This real exact crossing appears \emph{universal}: every Type-1 $N{=}3$ tested has
a real $u_*$ with two exactly degenerate eigenvalues ($99.8\%$ of $1290$ random samples within
$|u|\le 60$; the rest have the crossing beyond the box; the $\gamma$-signs are gauge). An analytic
proof of universality is open.\pnote{experiments/structural\_crossing.py; universality is a scan, not
yet a proof}

\subsection{Slope-free widths $\Rightarrow$ permanent overlap $\Rightarrow$ no factorization}
\textbf{R4 [EST].} The avoided-crossing width is slope-independent,
\begin{equation}
w_{ij}=\frac{2|V_{ij}|}{|a_i-a_j|}=|2s_{ij}|=\frac{2|\gamma_i\gamma_j|}{|\eps_i-\eps_j|},
\end{equation}
the slopes cancelling exactly.\pnote{coscaling\_derivation.md, symbolic} Both crossing separations
and widths scale as $\gamma^2/\Delta\eps$, so $\mathrm{sep}/w$ is a dimensionless shape function.
\textbf{R5 [NS].} On the genuine-LZ locus (all BE exponents $s_{ij}^2|a_i-a_j|=O(1)$) the closest
crossing pair is always within $\sim1.6$ widths ($35$k samples); \emph{at least two crossings are
permanently marginally overlapping}. Hence \textbf{no exact $\Scat=\prod_{i<j}\Scat_{ij}$ exists}; the Malikis--Cheianov
``isolated 2-level'' limit is unreachable in Type-1.\pnote{coscaling; ws\_d} \textbf{R6 [EST].}
Equivalently, Type-1 is \emph{never tridiagonal}: the extreme--extreme Cauchy coupling $V_{02}$ is
nonzero and \emph{dominant} (canonical $0.72>0.60>0.48$); every solvable cousin is tridiagonal.

\subsection{The joint and the function class}
\textbf{R7 [NS, verified].} The irreducible non-Abelian content is the holonomy of a single
$12{\times}13$ spectral-network \emph{joint}: in the overlapping regime the $12$ and $13$ Stokes
lines cross at four joints straddling the real axis, with the exact crossing between them; the joint
type ($12{\times}13$, sharing the middle level) \emph{is} $\Pmid$. Joint-free samples factorize
(enhancement $1.00\times$); jointed samples are enhanced ($120$--$130\times$ for sampleB).\pnote{ws\_g\_stokes\_graph.md;
figs/stokes\_\{sepA,overlapB\}.png; enhancement reproduced by oracle} \textbf{R8 [AD].} Two
independent derivations fix the function class. In the $u$-frame (WS-A) and in the Laplace frame
$\psi_j(u)=\int_C e^{-iuv}B_j(v)\,dv$, $B'(v)=-i\,\mathrm{diag}(1/a)(H_0-vI)B$ (WS-E), the scalar
connection problem has one finite \emph{accessory} singular point (anomalous indicial exponents
$\{0,1,3\}$, gap at $2$) plus the rank-2 irregular point: a genus-0 \emph{confluent-Heun} object,
\emph{one accessory parameter above} the ${}_1F_2$/Kamp\'e de F\'eriet of the solvable models. The
obstruction to a product/Bethe form is explicit and coordinator-verified: the Laplace-image matrices
do not commute, $[K(v_1),K(v_2)]=(v_1-v_2)[\mathrm{diag}(1/a)H_0,\mathrm{diag}(1/a)]\ne0$, the linear
model lacking the $1/t$ Coulomb term that renders the solvable cousins Fuchsian.\pnote{ws\_a +
ws\_e: two independent frames agree on confluent-Heun; the precise accessory-exponent count
$\{0,1,3\}$ is the WS-A/WS-E computation, not yet independently re-derived by the coordinator;
$[K,K]\ne0$ is coordinator-verified}

\subsection{The classifier}
\textbf{R9 [NS].} \emph{Elementary $\Leftrightarrow$ a level decouples.} The factorizable locus, the
joint-free locus, and the trivial-middle-coupling locus coincide and are the \emph{boundary} of the
genuine-LZ region (one middle coupling $\to0$); there is no interior elementary locus. Factorization
tracks the middle coupling, not the separation [Pearson$(\log(\text{enh}-1),\,\log$ of the smaller
middle BE exponent $s_{\rm mid,\cdot}^2|a_{\rm mid}-a_\cdot|){=}{+}0.51$ vs $\log(\mathrm{sep}/w){=}{+}0.015$].
The char-poly quadratic factor is irreducible over
$\mathbb{Q}(\gamma,\eps,a)$. BBGY's $\eps_2{=}\half(\eps_1{+}\eps_3)$ reduction does \emph{not}
transfer to the linear model.\pnote{ws\_c\_factorization\_locus.md} \textbf{R10 [NS].} The
prefactor is large and overlap-driven: $\Pmid/\Pmid^{\rm inc}=2.5$--$130\times$, pinned by a
$10^{-9}$ interaction-picture oracle (canonical $\Pmid{=}0.2147$, sampleB $0.0210$).\pnote{oracle\_report.md}

\section{The unifying picture}
The BE survivals are residue-collapsing periods of the phase one-form (elementary); the middle survival is the
holonomy of the one joint that genuine all-to-all coupling forces. ``Solvable'' MLZ models are
exactly those whose network is joint-free, which (R9) means a level decouples. Type-1 is the minimal
\emph{full} (non-tridiagonal) confluent system: its Cauchy structure (i) welds widths to level
spacing (R4) so crossings cannot separate, and (ii) keeps the extreme--extreme link dominant (R6),
guaranteeing the joint. Hence $\Pmid$ is an irreducible confluent-Heun Stokes coefficient.

\section{Predictions and domain}
P-i: the prefactor peaks as parameters approach a joint (overlap $\to$ large). P-ii [confirmed, R9]:
factorizable $=$ joint-free $=$ a level decouples. P-iii: the off-diagonal carries a relative-period
(open-path) phase, not a closed window period. \emph{Domain:} generic Type-1 $N{=}3$ (distinct
slopes, nonzero couplings); elementary only on the decoupling boundary. \emph{Breaks} (hardest) at
strong overlap, where the joint is deepest.

\section{Discussion: the open problem}
Everything is closed except the \emph{value} of the generic $\Pmid$: the connection coefficient of
the confluent-Heun (one-accessory-above-${}_1F_2$) problem of R8, with arguments fixed by the two
window actions and the turning-point cross-ratio. BBGY state the full $3\times3$ LZ connection is
``not solvable in terms of known special functions''; our results localize \emph{why} (the
$12{\times}13$ joint / the accessory point) and reduce the task to a single, well-posed connection
problem (companion: open-problem statement + plan-of-attack).
