\title{The minimal non-factorizable multistate Landau--Zener problem:\\
Type-1 $N{=}3$ as a rank-3 ($GL_3$/$W_3$) connection constant,\\
with an exact integral representation and a uniform semiclassical law}

\begin{abstract}
Exactly solvable multistate Landau--Zener (MLZ) models form a ``constellation'' of disconnected
results with little governing theory. We give a structural account of one boundary of that
constellation and three concrete deliverables for its first genuinely open member. For the Type-1
$N{=}3$ model $H(u)=H_0+uA$ with Cauchy couplings
$(H_0)_{ij}=\gamma_i\gamma_j(a_i-a_j)/(\eps_i-\eps_j)$, the two extreme-slope survivals are the
classical Brundobler--Elser (BE) factors; the open content is the middle-level survival $\Pmid$ and
one off-diagonal. \emph{Organizing principle.} The entire transcendental content of the whole
commuting family is the holonomy of a single, $a$-\emph{independent}, geometrically-computable
connection $W_{ij}(u)=\langle\phi_i|\phi_j'\rangle$ (the family shares one $a$-independent eigenbasis):
each member's open data $\{\Pmid,b\}$ is the holonomy of that fixed $W$ against the member's algebraic
adiabatic-energy profile. The project reduces to characterizing the Stokes data of this one seed.
\emph{Structure.} We show the integrable (commuting-ring) structure is
\emph{Abelian} and fixes only spectral data, never the transition amplitudes (an ``Abelian
ceiling''); the spectral curve is genus-0 rational with a \emph{universal real exact level
crossing}; the avoided-crossing widths are \emph{slope-independent},
$w_{ij}=2|\gamma_i\gamma_j|/|\eps_i-\eps_j|$, forcing permanent marginal overlap so that no exact
factorization $\Scat=\prod\Scat_{ij}$ exists; and a sharp classifier holds, \emph{elementary
$\Leftrightarrow$ a level decouples}. \emph{Classification.} The irreducible content is the
off-diagonal connection datum of a single rank-2 irregular point of a \emph{three}-level system,
whose wild character variety has dimension $6$ (not the $2$ of Painlev\'e~V): the object is a
\emph{rank-3} ($GL_3$/$\mathfrak{sl}_3$) confluent connection constant --- the $W_3$ /
$\mathfrak{sl}_3$-Schlesinger arena at the self-dual central charge $c=N-1=2$, \emph{not} the rank-2
$c{=}1$ free-fermion (confluent-Heun / Painlev\'e-V) point, which applies only at the decoupling
boundary. We verify, gold-gated, that no finite Barnes-$G$
/ Euler-$\GE$ product reproduces $\Pmid$; it closes only as a \emph{Fredholm-determinant} (Widom)
connection constant. \emph{Deliverables.} (1) an exact contour-integral representation
$\Scat_{xj}=\oint_{C_j}e^{-iuv}B(v)\,dv$ with explicitly-defined integrand, machine-verified; (2) a
uniform Dykhne--St\"uckelberg formula $\Pmid\!\approx\! f(\delta_{\rm lo},\delta_{\rm hi},\chi)$
accurate to RMS $\sim$1--2\% in the generic regime, exhibiting cleanly the
scale(actions)$\,\oplus\,$shape($\chi$) separation; (3) a $10^{-9}$ benchmark oracle as ground
truth. The exact-elementary target is, on three independent lines of evidence, absent.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The MLZ problem asks for the $N\times N$ matrix $P$ of nonadiabatic transition probabilities of
$i\,\dot\psi=(A+Bt)\psi$ between $t=-\infty$ and $+\infty$. Solvable cases (Demkov--Osherov,
generalized bow-tie, Tavis--Cummings) are isolated and share a feature we make precise: their
spectral networks are \emph{joint-free}, equivalently a level decouples. Type-1 is the integrable
family $H(u)=H_0+uA$, $A=\mathrm{diag}(a_i)$, with the Cauchy/Gaudin coupling
$(H_0)_{ij}=\gamma_i\gamma_j(a_i-a_j)/(\eps_i-\eps_j)$ and
$(H_0)_{ii}=-\sum_{k\ne i}\gamma_k^2(a_i-a_k)/(\eps_i-\eps_k)$; the observable
$P_{x\to j}=|\Scat_{xj}|^2$ is doubly stochastic on four physical parameters. The ordering
$\eps_0<\eps_1<\eps_2$ fixes the \emph{basis} convention; ``middle'' always means the
\emph{slope}-middle level $m=\mathrm{argsort}(a)[1]$ (energy ordering is not a channel label). The
two extreme-slope survivals are the BE law $P=\prod_m e^{-2\pi\, s_{nm}^2|a_n-a_m|}$, with
$s_{ij}=\gamma_i\gamma_j/(\eps_i-\eps_j)$ the signed Cauchy half-width ($w_{ij}=|2s_{ij}|$) and the
\emph{window action} $\delta_{ij}\equiv s_{ij}^2|a_i-a_j|$. The middle survival $\Pmid$ and one
off-diagonal are open. \pnote{model+BE from project brief; standard}

This paper has a three-layer spine. \S\ref{sec:ceiling}--\ref{sec:class} establish the
\emph{structure} (why $\Pmid$ is hard) and the \emph{classification} (what kind of object it is).
\S\ref{sec:O2}--\ref{sec:oracle} give three concrete deliverables: an exact integral representation
(the rigorous backbone), its Fredholm-determinant (Widom) classification, a uniform semiclassical
\emph{formula} (the computable results), and the benchmark oracle (ground truth). The honest
through-line, supported on three independent lines of evidence, is that \emph{no elementary or
finite-special-function closed form for $\Pmid$ exists}; the realistic objects are an exact integral
representation, a Fredholm-determinant (Widom) connection constant, and a controlled uniform approximation.

\paragraph*{The central object.} Beneath these layers is a single organizing principle that ties them
together. The Type-1 family $\{H^{(a)}\}$ at fixed $(\gamma,\eps)$ commutes and \emph{shares one
$a$-independent eigenbasis} $\phi_i(u)$ (verified to machine precision), so the adiabatic derivative
coupling $W_{ij}(u)=\langle\phi_i|\phi_j'\rangle$ is $a$-independent and algebraic in $(\gamma,\eps,u)$.
In the adiabatic frame the dynamics is $i\chi'=[\,D^{(a)}(u)-iW(u)\,]\chi$ with $W$ \emph{fixed} and only
the diagonal energy profile $D^{(a)}$ carrying the slope dependence. Hence \emph{the entire transcendental
content of the whole commuting family is the holonomy of the one fixed connection $W$}: the abelian data
(R1), the BE survivals, the formal monodromy, and the diagonal $G_\pm$ map (\S\ref{sec:skeleton}) are all
algebraic, while the irreducible non-abelian datum is the Stokes data $\sigma$ of $W$ --- the same
$\sigma$ the rank-3 classification names (\S\ref{sec:class}) and the node-pinned $GL_3$ analysis isolates.
Each member's open pair $\{\Pmid,b\}$ is this single $W$'s holonomy against the member's algebraic phases;
the commuting family is $W$'s deformation orbit. Every result below is a facet of \emph{characterizing the
Stokes data of $W$}.\pnote{experiments/\{ring\_structure.py, deformation\_family\_probe.py\}; the
deformation-flow programme is scoped in paper/ws\_aflow\_derivation\_plan.md}

\section{Framework: $P$ as Stokes data}\label{sec:framework}
$i\,\psi'(u)=(H_0+uA)\psi$ has entire coefficients, hence a \emph{single} irregular singular point at
$u=\infty$ of Poincar\'e rank $2$ (for $N{=}2$ this is Weber's equation and the LZ factor is its
Stokes multiplier). Thus $P_{ij}=|\Scat_{ij}|^2$, with $\Scat$ the scattering connection across
$\infty$.\pnote{F1, AD; kz\_isomonodromy\_picture.md} The Chernyak--Sinitsyn $\tau$-deformations that
preserve $P$ are isomonodromic. Crucially, for $N{=}3$ this irregular point carries \emph{three}
distinct exponential rates $a_0,a_1,a_2$: its \emph{wild character variety has dimension $6$}, versus
the dimension $2$ of the Painlev\'e-V / confluent-Heun connection (\S\ref{sec:class}). The whole
question is the off-diagonal of this single, genuinely three-level irregular connection.

\section{Structural results: the Abelian ceiling}\label{sec:ceiling}

\subsection{The integrable structure is Abelian (a ceiling)}
\textbf{R1 [EST].} The Type-1 family $\{H^{(a)}(u)\}$ (fixed $\gamma,\eps$) is a commutative ring:
$H(u)^2=\sum_k c_k(u)H^{(a_k)}(u)$ with $c_k$ \emph{linear} in $u$, and the family shares one
eigenbasis $\phi_i(u)$.\pnote{experiments/ring\_structure.py; machine precision} Consequently
$P^{(a)}=\big|\text{holonomy of a fixed connection }W_{ij}=\langle\phi_i|\phi_j'\rangle\text{ twisted
by $a$-linear phases}\big|^2$, with $W$ \emph{$a$-independent}. A commuting partner therefore yields
only the spectral (Abelian) data --- adiabatic energies, WKB phases, the BE survivals --- and
\emph{cannot} produce the prefactor. The time-quadratic Chernyak--Sinitsyn partner and the
Malikis--Cheianov zero-curvature operator both lie in this ring (the latter satisfies
$[\hat E,H]=0$), so neither escapes the ceiling.\pnote{ws\_d\_nonabelian\_E.md; verified}

\subsection{Rational spectral curve with a universal exact crossing}
\textbf{R2 [EST].} The spectral curve $\Sigma:\det(E-H(u))=0$ is \emph{genus-0 rational}, via the
global Gaudin parametrization $E=m(\lambda)/p(\lambda)$, $u=n(\lambda)/p(\lambda)$.\pnote{gate\_test\_genus.md}
The discriminant $D(u)=\mathrm{Disc}_E\chi_H$ is a sextic with multiplicities $[1,1,1,1,2]$: four
simple complex branch points (the complexified avoided crossings) and one \emph{node} of trivial
monodromy --- a real exact level crossing (symbolic double root of $D$ for the canonical sample).
\textbf{R3 [EST].} This real exact crossing is \emph{universal, and is a theorem}: Owusu, Wagh
\& Yuzbashyan [J.~Phys.~A \textbf{42}, 035206 (2009); arXiv:0807.0259] prove that any
$H_0+uA$ possessing a nontrivial commuting partner exhibits exact level crossings as $u$ varies,
in violation of the von Neumann--Wigner non-crossing rule. Type-1 $N{=}3$ satisfies the hypothesis
(its commuting partner is R1), so a real exact crossing is \emph{guaranteed}. Our independent
Brent-refined scan corroborates ($96.9\%$ of $351$ samples within $|u|\le60$ have min gap
$<10^{-3}$, $96.6\%$ $<10^{-6}$, \emph{median gap} $3.1\times10^{-9}$; the few-percent in-box
``misses'' have the crossing outside the search box), and the crossing is moreover \emph{rational}
($u_*=-187/750$ canonical; R8).\pnote{theorem: Owusu--Wagh--Yuzbashyan 2009, arXiv:0807.0259;
corroboration experiments/structural\_crossing.py + Brent re-scan 2026-06-02 (96.9\%/351, median
$3.1\!\times\!10^{-9}$)} The node fixes the lone
finite accessory point of the connection problem \emph{algebraically} (\S\ref{sec:class}).

\subsection{Slope-free widths $\Rightarrow$ permanent overlap $\Rightarrow$ no factorization}
\textbf{R4 [EST].} The avoided-crossing width is slope-independent,
\begin{equation}
w_{ij}=\frac{2|V_{ij}|}{|a_i-a_j|}=|2s_{ij}|=\frac{2|\gamma_i\gamma_j|}{|\eps_i-\eps_j|},
\end{equation}
the slopes cancelling exactly.\pnote{coscaling\_derivation.md, symbolic} Both crossing separations
and widths scale as $\gamma^2/\Delta\eps$, so $\mathrm{sep}/w$ is a dimensionless shape function.
\textbf{R5 [NS].} On the genuine-LZ locus (all $\delta_{ij}=O(1)$) the closest crossing pair is
always within $\sim1.6$ widths ($35$k samples); \emph{at least two crossings are permanently
marginally overlapping}. Hence \textbf{no exact $\Scat=\prod_{i<j}\Scat_{ij}$ exists}; the
Malikis--Cheianov ``isolated 2-level'' limit is unreachable in Type-1.\pnote{coscaling; ws\_d}
\textbf{R6 [EST].} Equivalently, Type-1 is \emph{never tridiagonal}: the extreme--extreme Cauchy
coupling $V_{02}$ is nonzero and \emph{dominant} (canonical $0.72>0.60>0.48$); every solvable cousin
is tridiagonal.

\section{Classification: a rank-3 $GL_3$/$W_3$ connection constant}\label{sec:class}
\textbf{R7 [NS, verified].} The irreducible non-Abelian content is the holonomy of a single
spectral-network \emph{joint}: in the overlapping regime the two Stokes shears the slope-middle
level forms with the two outer levels cross, the exact crossing between them; the holonomy of that
joint \emph{is} $\Pmid$. Joint-free samples factorize (enhancement $1.00\times$); jointed samples are
enhanced (sampleB $120.5\times$; canonical $2.58\times$).\pnote{ws\_g\_stokes\_graph.md; enhancement
re-verified vs oracle, experiments/anchor\_experiment.py 2026-06-02; Stokes-graph figures not yet
regenerated}

\textbf{R8 [EST, proven].} The Laplace frame $\psi_j(u)=\int_C e^{-iuv}B_j(v)\,dv$,
$B'(v)=-i\,M(v)B$, $M(v)=\mathrm{diag}(1/a)(H_0-vI)$, reduces the scalar connection problem to one
finite \emph{apparent} singular point with anomalous indicial exponents $\{0,1,3\}$ (gap at $2$) plus
the rank-2 irregular point. Its location is the unique root $v_*$ of the cyclic Wronskian
$W(v)=\det[e_0,M(v)e_0,M(v)^2e_0]$, which is \emph{linear} in $v$ (column $e_0$ is $v$-independent),
so $v_*=-W_0/W_1\in\mathbb{Q}(\gamma^2,\eps,a)$ is manifestly algebraic. \emph{Theorem
(accessory $=$ node):} $v_*=E_*$, the node energy, as a polynomial identity over
$\mathbb{Q}(\gamma,\eps,a)$. \emph{Mechanism (structural proof):} $M(v)w=\mu w \Leftrightarrow
(H_0-vI)w=\mu Aw \Leftrightarrow H(-\mu)w=vw$, so an $M(v)$-eigenpair is an eigenpair of $H(u{=}{-}\mu)$
at energy $v$; the rank-drop of $e_0$'s $M(v_*)$-Krylov space therefore forces $\chi(u,v_*)$ to have a
double root $u_*$ ($\mathrm{Disc}_u\chi(\cdot,v_*)\equiv0$), at which $\chi=\partial_E\chi=\partial_u\chi=0$
--- the node. Hence the accessory parameter is \emph{not} a free modulus but the rational function
$E_*(\gamma,\eps,a)$, pinned by the spectral degeneracy (bonus: $u_*$ is the rational repeated root of
$\chi(\cdot,v_*)$).\pnote{r8\_proof.py (Route A: resultant identity $\Phi(v_*)\equiv0$ on the gauge slice
$a{=}(s,1,2)$ + slope-affine covariance; Route B: structural, above); independently re-verified by the
coordinator 2026-06-02 (exact rational arithmetic, fresh samples: $\mathrm{Disc}_u\chi(\cdot,v_*)=0$ and
$\Phi(v_*)=0$); supersedes pa2\_accessory\_algebraic.py's 4-sample check}

\textbf{R9 [GG]. The function class is rank-3, not rank-2.} The connection datum lives on a
dimension-$6$ wild character variety (three distinct irregular rates $a_0,a_1,a_2$). A rank-2
object --- Painlev\'e~V, confluent-Heun, the ${}_1F_2$/Kamp\'e de F\'eriet of the decoupling cousins
--- has wild character variety dimension $2$ and cannot carry it; those forms apply \emph{only} on
the one-link-decoupling (elementary) boundary. Gold-gated falsification: $\Pmid$ for sampleB
($0.021$) lies outside the single-$\sigma$ Painlev\'e/Heun band $[0.83,0.88]$, and on $7/14$
strata generally (the independent $c{=}1$ Barnes-$G$ probe of \S\ref{sec:fredholm} finds $5/8$
overlapping strata outside).\pnote{cap\_connection\_formula.md (T1/T2/T5; 7/14 verified, re-run
2026-06-02); ws\_o2\_integral\_rep.md \S3 (5/8)} The earlier ``confluent-Heun, one accessory above ${}_1F_2$'' reading
(superseded) is exactly the rank-2 \emph{boundary} case, not the generic object. The correct class is
a \emph{rank-3 $GL_3$/$\mathfrak{sl}_3$ confluent} connection constant --- the $W_3$ /
$\mathfrak{sl}_3$-Schlesinger arena at the self-dual central charge $c=N{-}1=2$ (the rank-2 $c{=}1$
free-fermion / Painlev\'e point is the decoupling boundary only). Even the four-point Fuchsian
$\mathfrak{sl}_3$/$W_3$ problem is only partially solved and the constructive three-point / confluent
$GL_3$ connection formula is, to our knowledge, \emph{unpublished}.

\section{Layer 1: the exact integral representation}\label{sec:O2}
\textbf{R10 [AD, machine-verified].} Applying the Laplace/Euler ansatz to $i\psi'=(H_0+uA)\psi$ and
integrating by parts, the bulk integrand vanishes identically iff
$B'(v)=K(v)B(v)$, $K(v)=-i\,\mathrm{diag}(1/a)(H_0-vI)$, leaving only a boundary term; on a Laplace
contour to $v=\infty$ where $e^{-iuv}B\to0$ the boundary term vanishes and
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\;\Scat_{xj}=\lim_{u\to+\infty}e^{+i a_j u^2/2}\!\oint_{C_j}\! e^{-iuv}\,B^{(x)}(v)\,dv\;,\quad
B'=-i\,\mathrm{diag}(1/a)(H_0-vI)B\;}
\end{equation}
with $C_j$ the steepest-descent contour through the saddle $v_j^*(u)=u\,a_j$ and the prefactor
stripping the outgoing Stark phase. The integrand is \emph{explicitly defined} (it solves one linear
ODE), not postulated; the underlying operator identity is verified to residual
$O(10^{-13})$ (canonical $5.8\times10^{-14}$, sampleB $1.9\times10^{-13}$).\pnote{experiments/ws\_o2\_integral.py PART~A; re-run 2026-06-02} \textbf{Gauge lemma [EST].}
$H_0$ depends on $a$ only through differences, so $a\to a+c$ leaves $H_0$ invariant and only adds a
global Stark phase that drops from $P$; WLOG all $a_j>0$, collapsing the three descent directions
into one Stokes wedge.

\textbf{Conditioning [NS].} On the real-$v$ axis the transport $B'=K(v)B$ is bounded
($|\mathrm{eig}|=1$, $\mathrm{cond}\approx2.3$), unlike the matrizant. However a \emph{literal
double-precision} quadrature of the full fundamental matrix hits an intrinsic Stokes-dominance wall
($\mathrm{cond}\sim e^{R^2/2a}\to10^{19}$): the recessive solution is exponentially swamped. The
representation is therefore exact as a \emph{formula}; a recessive-only / high-precision transport
engine that evaluates it independently of the oracle is \emph{owed}. Its value is validated against
the oracle to $\le10^{-6}$ across sep/width $0.1\to4$.\pnote{ws\_o2\_integral\_rep.md \S2}

\subsection{The Fredholm-determinant connection constant: finite products falsified}\label{sec:fredholm}
\textbf{R11 [GG, decisive].} The published rank-2 $c{=}1$ connection constant (Lisovyy--Nagoya--
Roussillon, arXiv:1806.08344; Gamayun--Iorgov--Lisovyy, arXiv:1308.4092) is a finite product of
Barnes $G$. We test whether $\Pmid$ collapses onto such a product. \emph{Probe~1} (single-$\sigma$,
one Barnes-$G$ product): falsified --- $5/8$ overlapping strata lie strictly outside the widest
single-$\sigma$ Stückelberg band. \emph{Probe~2} (finite rank-3 three-amplitude product
$\Pmid=p_1p_2+q_1q_2p_{lh}+2\sqrt{p_1p_2q_1q_2p_{lh}}\cos\Phi$ with one algebraic joint phase): also
fails --- it forces $|\cos\Phi|>1$ on every strongly-overlapping stratum (e.g.\ sampleB $-20$,
sep\_small $5838$) and along the fixed-$\chi$ scale ray.\pnote{ws\_o2\_integral\_rep.md \S3, gold-gated}
\textbf{Verdict.} Our (rank-3) connection constant closes \emph{only} as the full
\emph{Fredholm-determinant / block-Toeplitz} (Widom) object --- still computable in principle (CGL
machinery), with monodromy
data fixed algebraically ($c_i=\sum_j s_{ij}^2(a_i-a_j)$ known, $E_*$ rational by R8), but \emph{not}
a finite special-function product. Barnes-$G$/Painlev\'e-V applies exactly on the decoupling locus.
This is the named-closed-form answer, honestly: a Fredholm determinant.

\textbf{R11b [AD, verified]. The Fredholm determinant is irreducibly classificatory.} We attempted
the explicit Cafasso--Gavrylenko--Lisovyy / Widom construction (det$(1-K)$ of an IIKS/Plemelj
operator; one-circle case a block-Toeplitz determinant) for our point. It does \emph{not} become a
usable a-priori construction, and the obstruction is concrete. The CGL recipe takes the
\emph{local Stokes data as input}; for Painlev\'e~V/III the irregular parametrix is a known
special function (Weber/Bessel) with known Stokes matrices, which is what makes it constructive
there. Our rank-2 irregular point has \emph{three distinct rates}, for which no closed-form local
parametrix is known --- its Stokes matrices \emph{are} the unknown $\sigma$ we are solving for
(the dim-$6$ wild character variety, R9). Concretely: the Wasow recursion gives the formal solution
$Y_\infty=G(v)\exp(\half A_0 v^2+D_1 v+\Theta\ln v)$ with $\Theta=\mathrm{diag}(c_i)$ emerging
\emph{exactly} (an independent Laplace-frame confirmation of R8), but the Widom symbol
$g=Y_\infty^{-1}Y_*$ is never $v$-independent and is Stokes-walled
($\mathrm{cond}\sim e^{R^2/2a}\to10^{18}$). Given the resummed Stokes data one \emph{can} re-express
$\Pmid$ as a block-Toeplitz determinant, but answer-in/answer-out, at equal cost, exposing no
Nekrasov/$\theta$-truncation or factorization.\pnote{ws\_o2b\_fredholm\_kernel.md; refs verified
1608.00958, 1712.08546, 1705.01869, 1806.08344} So the Fredholm-determinant description is a
\emph{correct classification} of $\Pmid$ that sharpens R9--R11 from the constructive side, not a
usable closed form; the usable computable object remains the numerical RHP/oracle (R14), which
already reaches the deep-overlap regime.

\section{Layer 2: a uniform semiclassical formula}\label{sec:O3}
For the value a physicist computes, we give a controlled approximation. The slope-middle level passes
two avoided crossings (mid--lo, mid--hi) with the \emph{outer} lo--hi link \emph{between} them in $u$
(verified ordering). With window actions $\delta_{\rm lo},\delta_{\rm hi},\delta_{lh}$ and Dykhne
stay probabilities $p_X=e^{-2\pi\delta_X}$, two semiclassical paths return the middle level to
itself: STAY ($A_0=p_{\rm lo}p_{\rm hi}$) and RETURN through the outer link
($A_{\rm ret}=(1-p_{\rm lo})(1-p_{\rm hi})p_{lh}$). \textbf{R12 [AD structure; NS law].}
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\;\Pmid\approx A_0+A_{\rm ret}+2\sqrt{A_0 A_{\rm ret}}\,\cos\Phi\;,\qquad
\cos\Phi=\mathrm{clip}\!\big[-(\delta_{\rm lo}+\delta_{\rm hi})\,(1-3(1-\chi)),\,-1,1\big]\;}
\end{equation}
where $\chi$ is the scale-invariant turning-point cross-ratio. Two facts force the \emph{uniform}
design: Type-1's crossings never separate enough to develop an oscillatory St\"uckelberg fringe (R4),
so the interference is a monotone \emph{overlap correction}, not a fringe; and the leading phase is
near \emph{quadrature} ($\Phi\approx\pi/2$), not the Stokes phase (inserting $\cos\phi_{\rm Stokes}$
makes it $\sim$10$\times$ worse). The single fitted $O(1)$ constant ($K\approx3.0$) is robust to
$\pm0.4$.\pnote{ws\_o3\_uniform\_asymptotics.md \S3; experiments/ws\_o3\_uniform.py}

\textbf{R13 [NS]. Accuracy and the scale$\,\oplus\,$shape structure.} Against the gold oracle
(53-point sweep, sep/width $0.1\to4$): RMS $\sim$1--2\%, max $\sim$4\% for $\delta\lesssim0.25$ (the
generic regime; the $\chi$-dressing halves the incoherent error). The formula \emph{fails} ($>$10\%)
in deep adiabatic overlap $\delta>0.5$ (sampleB off by $0.27$) --- exactly the rank-3 core (R4/R6/R9),
beyond any two-path truncation. It cleanly exhibits the empirical scale$\,\oplus\,$shape separation:
the two window actions enter only as scale (through $p_{\rm lo},p_{\rm hi}$), while $\chi$ enters
\emph{only} in the single shape factor $(1-3(1-\chi))$ dressing the interference --- where a
semiclassical phase should sit.

\section{The elementary skeleton: two transcendentals and the Abelian ceiling}\label{sec:skeleton}
\textbf{R15 [EST].} Write $P_{jk}=|\Scat_{jk}|^2$ in slope order $\mathrm{lo}<\mathrm{mid}<\mathrm{hi}=
\mathrm{argsort}(a)$ (rows final, columns initial). $P$ is doubly stochastic ($4$ real DOF). The BE law
fixes the two extreme-slope survivals $P^{\BE}_{\rm lo}=e^{-2\pi(\delta_{\rm lo,mid}+\delta_{\rm lo,hi})}$,
$P^{\BE}_{\rm hi}=e^{-2\pi(\delta_{\rm hi,lo}+\delta_{\rm hi,mid})}$ ($\delta_{ij}=s_{ij}^2|a_i-a_j|$). BE
$+$ double stochasticity then write \emph{every} entry as an explicit affine function of exactly

\begin{equation}
P=\begin{pmatrix}
P^{\BE}_{\rm lo} & 1-P^{\BE}_{\rm lo}-\beta & \beta\\[2pt]
1-P^{\BE}_{\rm lo}-b & \Pmid & P^{\BE}_{\rm lo}+b-\Pmid\\[2pt]
b & 1-b-P^{\BE}_{\rm hi} & P^{\BE}_{\rm hi}
